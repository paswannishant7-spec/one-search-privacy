# Required business information

Do not remove draft status, enable indexing, or submit the URL to an app store until these items are supplied, verified, and approved.

## Controller and contact

- [ ] Full legal entity name and business form.
- [ ] Registered office and legally required service address.
- [ ] Country of establishment.
- [ ] Monitored privacy email on a controlled domain.
- [ ] Grievance officer/contact required for India, with approved publication details.
- [ ] Data protection officer or representative, if applicable.
- [ ] Support and security-reporting channels with named operational owners.

## Product scope

- [ ] Android and iOS app names, package/bundle IDs, version, and release channel.
- [ ] Launch countries, languages, intended audience, and minimum age.
- [ ] Complete live feature list; distinguish shipped, beta, and planned features.
- [ ] Whether users create accounts and all authentication methods.
- [ ] Whether users upload photos, videos, audio, or files.
- [ ] Exact face detection, face swap, likeness, or identity functionality.
- [ ] Monetization model, subscriptions, ads, analytics, attribution, and tracking.
- [ ] User-generated/public content, sharing, reporting, blocking, and moderation behavior.

## Vendors and operations

- [ ] Cloud, database, object storage, CDN, email, support, and observability providers.
- [ ] Every mobile SDK and exact configuration.
- [ ] Every AI/model provider, region, training setting, retention term, and subprocessor.
- [ ] Payment and receipt-validation providers.
- [ ] Cross-border transfer map and contractual safeguards.
- [ ] Data retention schedule with technical owners and enforcement evidence.
- [ ] Security controls approved for public description.
- [ ] Incident response, government-request, and data-rights procedures.

## Approvals

- [ ] Product and engineering certify the data inventory against production builds.
- [ ] Security approves safeguard statements and vendor risks.
- [ ] Business approves operator/contact facts and launch regions.
- [ ] Legal counsel reviews applicable laws, consent, age, biometric/likeness, AI, transfers, and final copy.
- [ ] Store owners reconcile disclosures and archive evidence.
